## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an organic dialogue between modern culinary warmth and tactile, non-intrusive ambient computing. Designed for a spatial AI kitchen companion, it resolves the friction between messy, flour-dusted hands and digital interactions. 

The aesthetic merges **Warm Scandinavian Kitchen Modernism** with **Soft Ambient AI**. Instead of cold laboratory glass or aggressive cybernetic cues, the system evokes sunlit ceramic countertops, cast-iron cookware, and heirloom linen textures, subtly augmented by ethereal, responsive light states.

### Key Tenets:
- **Glanceable & Calm:** Kitchens are sensory environments; the interface delivers high legibility and instant gesture-feedback states without visual clutter.
- **Physicality Meets Optics:** High-touch digital components mirror bespoke ceramic and stone finishes, countered by faint, diffused light halos signaling gesture recognition and system perception.
- **Sensory Reassurance:** Transitions feel soft, fluid, and predictable, creating an intuitive mental model for touchless interaction.

## Colors

The chromatic language roots technical innovation in culinary materiality. High-energy actions and active states lean into fiery earth, while structural hierarchy and environmental surfaces draw from natural unbleached textiles and mineral shades.

- **Primary (`#D96236` — Terracotta Hearth):** Evokes seasoned terracotta, simmering reduction glazes, and kitchen vitality. Used strictly for dominant calls-to-action, primary interactive affordances, and key state transitions.
- **Secondary (`#5B6B54` — Kitchen Sage):** Anchors secondary features, ingredient tags, recipe complexity ratings, and passive hardware status. Provides balanced visual reprieve.
- **Tertiary (`#42D9C8` — Ambient Cyan Halo):** The voice of the AI. Reserved exclusively for gesture-detection triggers, camera field-of-view indicators, active listening states, and optical confirmation glows.
- **Neutral (`#272523` — Roasted Peppercorn):** A softened, warm-charcoal base providing rich contrast without the sterility of pure black (`#000000`).

### Surface Palette
- **Canvas / Root Background:** Warm unglazed porcelain (`#FBF8F4`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card/Container):** Whipped buttermilk linen (`#F4EFE6`).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Floating Popovers/Modals):** High-opacity warm porcelain tint (`#ECE4D6`).
- **Border / Subtle Division:** Low-contrast bone ash (`#E3D9C9`).

## Typography

Typographic scale and hierarchy are driven by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its geometric foundation softened by generous apertures and organic terminal curves. This typeface balances the clean precision of high-end consumer technology with the welcoming humanity of a bespoke lifestyle journal.

### Design Directives:
- **Arm’s-Length Glanceability:** Step instructions and live timer readouts rely on heavy font weights (`600` and `700`) paired with open tracking to remain effortless to parse when resting on a counter standard distances away.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Display levels celebrate spacious line heights, avoiding dense text clusters. Text links and metadata tags leverage crisp medium weights (`500` / `600`) with modest letter spacing (`+0.01em`) to maintain structural sharpness.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built around a mobile-first, edge-cushioned fluid grid prioritizing thumb access and single-handed interaction.

### Breakpoints & Columns
- **Mobile (Base, `< 640px`):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` column gutters. Interactive action triggers occupy full-width tap zones or large floating bottom navigation bars.
- **Tablet (`640px – 1024px`):** 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins. Content splits into recipe progression streams and live device preview panes.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column structured grid centered within a `1200px` max-width container, featuring asymmetrical layout compositions that balance large hardware photography with editorial feature narratives.

### Rhythmic Discipline
Vertical pacing strictly follows an 8-point rhythm scaled via standard spacing tokens (`0.25rem` to `2.5rem`). Touch targets enforce a minimum clearance of `48px × 48px` to guarantee zero-friction touch interaction when gesture control is disengaged.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids harsh artificial drop shadows, utilizing **Ambient Clay Diffusion** to simulate warm overhead kitchen downlighting falling across matte surfaces.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Surface is flush with `#FBF8F4`. Separation occurs purely through fine hairline boundaries (`1px solid #E3D9C9`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Soft, tinted ambient shadow mimicking warm counter reflections: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(78, 59, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(78, 59, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Assistive HUD & Gestural Menus):** Elevated floating modules suspended over active live feeds: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(60, 44, 30, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(60, 44, 30, 0.06)`.
- **Active AI Emissive State (Glow Layer):** Overlays an optical field: `0 0 24px -2px rgba(66, 217, 200, 0.35)`. Signifies that the camera sensor has locked onto hand coordinates and is receiving live spatial commands.

## Shapes

The spatial curvature matches smooth, tumbling stoneware ceramics and ergonomic molded camera chassis. 

### Geometric Rationale
- **Base Components (`roundedness: 2` = 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to compact interior elements such as inputs, badges, data tables, and mini metrics.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg` = 1rem / 16px):** Standard recipe steps, hardware feature cards, and camera feed frames.
- **Hero Containers & Sticky Trays (`rounded-xl` = 1.5rem / 24px):** Major narrative modules, modals, and edge sheets.
- **Pill Attributes (Fully Rounded):** Applied selectively to interactive state chips, mode switches, and gesture validation indicators to reinforce physical hardware pushbuttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Terracotta (`#D96236`) surface with high-contrast warm porcelain (`#FBF8F4`) typography. Generous padding (`16px 24px`), bold weight, pill-shaped or `rounded-lg`. On tap/hover, darkens smoothly to `#BF5129`.
- **Secondary (Kitchen Craft):** Bordered frame (`1.5px solid #5B6B54`) over transparent or light linen surface with sage typography.
- **AI Indicator Ghost Button:** Translucent backing (`rgba(66, 217, 200, 0.08)`) with a fine border (`1px solid #42D9C8`) and glowing cursor-tracking state.

### Chips & Status Tags
- **Dietary & Gesture Tags:** Compact, rounded pills utilizing softened herb tones (Sage `#5B6B54` at 10% opacity with solid `#3E4D38` text).
- **Vision Feed Status Chip:** Pulsing tertiary cyan ring with an internal `#42D9C8` dot indicating active gesture capture ("Wave to Scroll Active").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Cooking Notes & Search Bars:** Background filled with `#F4EFE6`, borderless in resting state. Transition into a crisp `1.5px solid #D96236` focus frame with zero harsh blue outlines. Floating labels rendered in `body-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Step Completion
- Circular check elements referencing traditional timers. Checked states fill with `#5B6B54` and display an off-white organic check icon, automatically striking through completed recipe directions.

### Recipe & Vision Cards
- Structured with `#F4EFE6` linen fill, soft Level 1 ambient elevation, and a distinct gesture-activation target area. When an overhead hand gesture is detected, cards emit an ambient `#42D9C8` boundary aura.

### Gesture HUD Overlay (Specialized Component)
- Mobile screen top/bottom anchored floating HUD showing recognized spatial commands (e.g., *Palm Hold: Pause Timer*, *Swipe Right: Next Step*). Rendered in frosted linen glass (`background: rgba(244, 239, 230, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with subtle cyan edge accents.
